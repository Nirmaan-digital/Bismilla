Privacy Policy

Bismillah Chicken Center · Last updated 25 October 2026

This policy explains how Bismillah Chicken Center ("we", "us") handles information in the Bismillah Chicken Center Android app and the website bismillahchickencenter.com (together, the "Service"). The Service is used by our business: our retailers (shops that buy from us), delivery drivers, staff and administrators. Accounts are created by us; there is no public sign-up.

Information we collect

  • Account details: name, phone number, email address (optional) and a password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Retailer details: shop name, owner name, shop address, area, city and pincode.
  • Driver details: name, phone number, vehicle number and type, and driving licence number.
  • Business records: orders, deliveries, trips, prices, payments, outstanding balances and expenses.
  • Photos you upload: drivers can upload photos of trip bills (for example diesel bills). The camera or photo picker is used only when you choose to add a photo.
  • Payment information: online payments are processed by our payment provider (Razorpay or Stripe). We receive the payment status and reference, never your full card or bank details.
  • Notification token: if you allow notifications, we store a push token for your device so we can send you alerts.
  • Technical data: basic server logs (such as IP address, time and the page requested) used for security and fixing problems.

We do not collect your location, contacts, call logs or SMS, and we do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use information

  • To run the Service: take and deliver orders, plan trips, record payments and keep accounts.
  • To send notifications about price updates, new orders, assigned trips, deliveries and payments.
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent misuse and fix problems.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting requirements.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that help us run the Service: our hosting provider (Hostinger), Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and browser push services (for notifications), and Razorpay or Stripe (for payments). They may use the information only to provide their service to us.
  • People in our business who need it: for example, a driver sees the retailer's shop name, address and phone number for a delivery.
  • Authorities when the law requires it.

Storage on your device

So the app and website can open without internet, a copy of pages you have viewed is kept on your device. It is removed when you log out.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored as secure hashes, and access is limited by account role.

Retention

We keep account and business records while your account is active and afterwards for as long as needed for legal, tax and accounting purposes. Notification tokens are removed when you log out or when they stop working.

Your choices and deleting your account

  • You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone or browser settings.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information.
  • To delete your account, email support@bismillahchickencenter.com from your registered email, or ask us from your registered phone number, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete your account and personal details within 30 days. Records we must keep by law (such as invoices and payment records) are kept for the required period and then deleted.

Children

The Service is for business use and is not meant for children under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date.
